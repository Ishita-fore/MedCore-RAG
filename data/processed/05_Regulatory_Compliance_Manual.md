## 1. Purpose and Regulatory Governance

This manual establishes a practical compliance framework for hospital operations. It maps common regulatory obligations to accountable functions, evidence requirements, monitoring activities and escalation routes.

Regulatory requirements vary by service, jurisdiction and activity. Where a specific law, regulator direction, contractual requirement or formal legal advice imposes a stricter requirement, that requirement takes precedence.

## 2. Compliance Operating Model

| Layer               | Owner                       | Primary activity                                                  |
|---------------------|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Policy              | Legal & Compliance          | Maintain policies, interpret obligations, advise business owners. |
| Operational control | Department heads            | Embed requirements into daily workflows.                          |
| Monitoring          | Compliance / Internal Audit | Test controls, review evidence, track findings.                   |
| Remediation         | Control owner               | Correct gaps, assign actions and document closure.                |
| Governance          | Executive / committee       | Review material risks, incidents and overdue actions.             |

## 3. Core Compliance Domains

| Domain                   | Typical requirements                                   | Evidence examples                                 |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Patient rights & consent | Consent, communication, grievance handling             | Consent forms, logs, case records                 |
| Privacy & records        | Confidentiality, access, disclosure, retention         | Access logs, release registers, retention records |
| Clinical governance      | Approved procedures, incident review, quality controls | Audits, committee minutes, corrective actions     |

| Workforce                | Training, credentials, conduct                         | Training logs, credential files                   |

| Information security     | Access, incident response, system safeguards           | Access reviews, incident tickets, audit logs      |

## 4. Regulatory Change Management

Legal &amp; Compliance maintains a regulatory watch process and assesses material changes for applicability. When a change affects a control, the accountable owner documents the gap, required action, owner and target date.

Changes should be reflected in controlled policies, procedures, training and system configurations where applicable. Superseded versions must be retained according to document-control requirements.

## 5. Compliance Evidence

Evidence must be attributable, dated where relevant, retrievable and protected from unauthorized alteration. Screenshots or extracts should include enough context to demonstrate what was tested.

Where a control is not applicable, the owner should document the basis rather than leaving the control field blank.

## 6. Incident, Breach and Non-Compliance Escalation

Potential privacy incidents, safety concerns, material financial irregularities, regulatory notices and repeated control failures must be escalated promptly to the designated owner and Legal/Compliance.

Escalation does not by itself determine legal reporting obligations. The responsible teams assess facts, applicable requirements, notification deadlines and evidence.

## 7. Monitoring and Internal Review

| Review type        | Typical frequency       | Purpose                                                 |
|--------------------|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Control self-check | Monthly / quarterly     | Confirm key controls are operating.                     |
| Compliance review  | Quarterly               | Test selected domains and evidence.                     |
| Internal audit     | Risk-based              | Independent assessment of control design and operation. |
| Management review  | Quarterly / as required | Review material risks, incidents and remediation.       |

## 8. Findings and Corrective Actions

Each material finding should have a documented owner, root-cause assessment, corrective action, target date and closure evidence. Extensions should be approved by the relevant authority and documented.

Closure requires evidence that the agreed action was completed and, where appropriate, that the control is operating effectively.

## 9. Training and Awareness

Personnel receive training appropriate to their role and the information or processes they handle. High-risk roles may require additional role-specific training.

Managers should address recurring errors through coaching, workflow improvement and targeted refresher training rather than relying solely on policy distribution.

## 10. Manual Review and Governance

This manual is reviewed at least annually and when significant legal, regulatory, organizational or technology changes occur. The Compliance Reporting Matrix is used to maintain ownership, evidence and review status.
