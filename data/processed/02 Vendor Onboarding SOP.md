## Vendor Onboarding Standard Operating Procedure

Controlled workflow for supplier registration, due diligence and activation

|----------------|-------------------------|
| Version        | 2.0                     |
| Effective Date | 01 September 2026       |
| Review Cycle   | Annual                  |
| Classification | Internal - Confidential |

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for registering, screening, approving and activating vendors before they are used for MedCore purchases.

## 2. Scope

Applies to new vendors, material changes to existing vendors, high-risk service providers and suppliers requiring access to MedCore premises, systems, data or patient-facing operations.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role                  | Responsibility                                                                       |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Requesting Department | Defines business need, scope, technical requirements and proposed supplier.          |
| Procurement           | Coordinates sourcing, onboarding documentation, commercial checks and vendor record. |
| Finance               | Validates banking/tax information and payment setup requirements.                    |
| IT / Security         | Performs security review when systems, data or connectivity are involved.            |
| Legal & Compliance    | Reviews higher-risk contractual, regulatory, privacy or compliance matters.          |
| Approver              | Confirms business need, budget and risk acceptance at the required authority level.  |

## 4. Standard Onboarding Workflow

1. Business owner submits vendor request and business justification.
2. Procurement checks whether an approved vendor already exists and determines the applicable sourcing route.
3. Vendor submits required corporate, tax, banking, service and compliance information.
4. Applicable risk checks are completed, including information-security or privacy review where relevant.
5. Procurement and the responsible approver review the completed package.
6. Finance creates or activates the supplier record after validation.
7. Contract / purchase order is issued before operational engagement, unless an approved exception applies.

## 5. Minimum Vendor Documentation

| Document / information                     | Applicability   |
|--------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Legal entity name and registration details | All vendors     |

| Document / information                       | Applicability                                     |
|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Tax and invoicing information                | All vendors                                       |
| Banking / payment details                    | Vendors receiving payment                         |
| Scope, quotation or proposal                 | All material purchases                            |
| Signed contract / terms                      | Where required by procurement or legal assessment |
| Compliance declaration / conflict disclosure | Risk-based                                        |
| Security / privacy questionnaire             | Vendors handling systems or sensitive information |
| Insurance / certifications                   | Where required by service or risk profile         |

## 6. Due Diligence and Risk Classification

| Risk   | Typical characteristics                                                             | Additional review                                                      |
|--------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Low    | Routine goods, no sensitive access, low operational impact                          | Standard documentation and commercial review                           |
| Medium | Operational dependency, specialist services, moderate access                        | Enhanced due diligence and performance terms                           |
| High   | Sensitive data, critical technology, patient-facing or major operational dependency | Legal/Compliance and/or IT Security review; stronger contract controls |

## 7. Bank Detail Verification

Changes to vendor bank details require verification through the approved finance control. Bank information received by email should not be changed solely on the basis of an unsolicited request.

Potential payment-fraud indicators must be escalated to Finance and the relevant control owner before payment details are changed.

## 8. Activation and Periodic Review

Vendor activation occurs only after required approvals and validations are complete. High-risk vendors may require periodic reassessment, updated documentation and performance review.

Inactive or obsolete vendor records should be restricted or deactivated according to Finance and Procurement procedures.

## 9. Exceptions and Escalation

Emergency onboarding may be permitted when delay creates a material operational or patient-care risk. The exception must document the reason, approvals, temporary controls and follow-up actions.

Missing or inconsistent documentation should be resolved before activation unless a formally approved exception applies.
